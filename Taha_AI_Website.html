01 / The big idea

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that usually require human-like abilities, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making predictions.

📚

Learn from data

AI systems can find patterns in examples and use them to improve a task.

🔎

Recognize patterns

AI can help identify objects in images, speech in audio, or trends in information.

🧩

Support decisions

AI can suggest options or predictions, while people remain responsible for decisions.

AI is a tool built by people. It can be powerful, but it does not think or understand exactly like a human.
02 / How it works

From data to results

Many AI systems are trained on examples. During training, a model learns patterns that help it produce a prediction, recommendation, or response.

STEP 01Collect data

Gather useful examples, while respecting privacy and permissions.

STEP 02Train a model

Algorithms adjust the model to learn patterns from those examples.

STEP 03Test it

Check how well it works, including on examples it has not seen.

STEP 04Use responsibly

Monitor results and let people review important decisions.

Example: a photo app may learn visual patterns from labelled images to help sort pictures by subject.
03 / Everyday life

AI is already around us

You may use AI more often than you realize. Here are a few everyday examples.

🗣️

Voice assistants

Recognize spoken requests and respond to questions or commands.

🎬

Recommendations

Suggest videos, music, or products based on activity and preferences.

🗺️

Navigation

Estimate travel times and help find routes using changing traffic data.

🌐

Translation

Help translate text and speech between different languages.

🩺

Healthcare

Assist trained professionals with tasks such as analysing medical images.

🎓

Education

Offer explanations, practice questions, and learning support.

04 / The opportunities

Why does AI matter?

✨ Potential benefits

✓Automates repetitive tasks and can save time.
✓Helps people explore large amounts of information.
✓Can improve accessibility through captions and assistive tools.
✓Supports research and helps generate new ideas.

⚠️ Challenges to consider

!AI can make mistakes or provide made-up information.
!Biased data can lead to unfair outcomes.
!Personal data and privacy need protection.
!Some tasks and jobs may change as AI develops.
05 / Responsible use

Use AI wisely

The goal is not simply to use more AI. It is to use it thoughtfully, fairly, and safely.

✅

Verify facts

Check important answers against trusted sources and your class materials.

🔐

Protect privacy

Do not share passwords, private details, or someone else's personal information.

🧑‍⚖️

Keep humans involved

Use human judgment for important choices and be honest about AI assistance.

Remember: AI can assist your thinking, but it should not replace your own understanding.
06 / Looking ahead

What could the future bring?

AI may become more useful in science, learning, accessibility, transport, and creative work. Its impact will depend on the choices people make today.

Skills for the future

1Critical thinking: question and evaluate results.
2Creativity: bring original ideas and perspectives.
3Digital literacy: understand tools and their limits.
4Empathy and teamwork: work well with people.

Key message

The future of AI is not only about smarter machines. It is also about responsible people making good choices.

Progress should benefit people while reducing risks and unfairness.
